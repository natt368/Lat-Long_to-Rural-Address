Rural Address Lookup

Paste GPS coordinates (like the ones Google Maps copies) to find the rural address for BC, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, or Ontario — e.g. 9001 Township Road 365.

Dominion Land Survey grid

Computed from the official survey grid where available (Alberta, Saskatchewan). The legal location is exact; civic (911) numbers are estimated from each province's standard formula and may differ slightly from the number on the actual sign. Manitoba, BC, and Ontario aren't covered by a consistent province-wide grid addressing system, so only the OpenStreetMap result below applies there.
